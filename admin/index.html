Access

Members

Method A — Google sign-in (recommended for most members)

  1. Admin: Click + Add member, enter name and email, click Save
  2. Member receives: A welcome email from your Gmail with a link to the workspace
  3. Member: Clicks the link, signs in with their Google account — done
  4. No username or password needed. No Firebase email.

Method B — Passwordless magic link (for members who cannot use Google)

  1. Admin: Click + Add member, enter name and email, check "Also send login link", click Save
  2. Member receives two emails:
    • Welcome email: Your welcome email from Gmail
    • Magic link: A one-click login link from Firebase (noreply@the-untaught-lessons.firebaseapp.com)
  3. Member: Clicks the Firebase link — lands directly in the workspace
  4. The Firebase link expires after 24 hours. If it expires, use "Send login link" in the table row.

Method C — Emergency access (admin/owner only, not for regular members)

  1. There is no username/password option for regular members — the sign-in page only ever offers Google, Microsoft, Facebook, and the magic link above.
  2. A real password can be set for a break-glass account from the Emergency access tab, for the rare case all other sign-in methods are down. The email must already be an active admin or owner here in Members.
  3. Sign in with it at /member-login/?emergency=1 — this URL is not linked from anywhere on the site.

Removing a member

  1. Admin: Find the member in the table, click Remove
  2. Automated: Removed from Firestore member list and user profile — their next page load will be rejected
  3. Active sessions last up to 1 hour before expiring. Outstanding login links cannot be remotely revoked.

How the assessment works

The same assessment structure is used twice: the diagnostic before the program and the checkpoint after it. A learner receives one of three scenario forms. Think is worth 40 points, Speak 30, and Act 30, for a total of 100.

Part 1a asks the learner to organize information. Part 1b tests whether they can diagnose, extend, and choose a logical structure. Part 2 asks for a concise recommendation based on supplied facts. Part 3 asks for a decision, a response to pushback, and a next step.

Think is always scored from keyed answers. Speak and Act use the deterministic C³ rules below unless their independent GenAI toggle is enabled and returns a valid score. When GenAI is enabled, deterministic scoring also runs and the difference is logged privately for calibration. Learners see only the official score, and GenAI failure silently falls back to deterministic scoring.

Part 1a · Organize informationThree form-specific sorting exercises · 20 points

Learner instructions and scoring

Organize the nine tasks according to when they should be handled. Choose the best three timing headings from six options, then place every task under one heading. The timing rule is stated explicitly so the exercise tests whether the learner can build and apply that structure—not whether they happen to choose the same organizing dimension as the assessment author. Each correct heading used once earns 2 points, for 6 points total. Correct placement is worth 14 points divided equally across nine items. The Part 1a result is rounded to one decimal and capped at 20.

Learner-view preview: use the button on any form card below to complete that exact sorting exercise. Preview answers are temporary and are never saved as learner results.

Part 1b · Test the structure45-question bank · 15 questions drawn per attempt · 20 points

Learner instructions and scoring

Answer 15 multiple-choice questions in mixed order: five from each format. Each correct answer earns 20 ÷ 15 points, or about 1.33 points. Incorrect answers receive zero; there is no partial credit. The Part 1b score is capped at 20.

Part 2 · Speak conciselyThree form-specific recommendation scenarios · 30 points

C³ scoring

The learner recommends one option, supports it with scenario-based reasoning, and gives a practical next step. No option is keyed or preferred: a well-supported alternative can earn full credit. Typed and transcribed responses use the same content rules; punctuation and vocal delivery are not scored.

  • Leads — 8 points: identifiable recommendation, clarity, and early placement.
  • Supports — 14 points: up to 9 for distinct scenario concepts and 5 for reasoning linkage; bare keyword lists do not earn full credit.
  • Focuses — 8 points: up to 5 for a practical next action and 3 for broad length/focus.
  • Safeguard: without an identifiable recommendation, the total is capped at 10.
Part 3 · Act with judgmentThree form-specific decisions and pushback scenarios · 30 points

C³ scoring

The learner chooses a first action, commits to it, adapts to pushback, and advances the situation. Every listed action is treated equally; the reference default is not required for full credit. Typed and transcribed responses use the same content rules; vocal delivery is not scored.

  • Decides — 10 points: action selection 3, explicit commitment 4, and scenario-linked rationale 3.
  • Adapts — 12 points: concern recognition 5, mitigation 4, and connection to the decision/trade-off 3.
  • Advances — 8 points: concrete next action 5, ownership/timing specificity 2, and broad length/focus 1.
  • Safeguard: without an explicit decision, the total is capped at 8.
