Who this is for

This program was built for people who are done waiting to be taught.

Students

Grades are only part of what opens a great opportunity. You also need to explain your thinking clearly and stay steady under pressure. Think, speak and act like an executive builds exactly those skills, the ones universities and employers look for but few schools ever teach.

Professionals

You already have the experience and the ideas. The harder part is explaining them to the people who decide. In Think, speak and act like an executive, you practice organizing your thinking and making your case under pressure.

Organizations

Technical skill alone does not prepare a team for a tense meeting or a difficult decision. People still need to think clearly, state the point and lead without hedging. Think, speak and act like an executive gives the team a shared way to structure a problem and discuss what to do.

AI-powered practice

Every phase includes practice with AI.

You work through each problem yourself first. Then you use AI to pressure-test your logic, sharpen your wording, and rehearse the moment before it counts.

Phase 1 · Think clearly

Use AI as a thinking partner. Stress-test your logic, identify gaps in your reasoning, and compress your ideas before you communicate them.

Phase 2 · Speak concisely

Use AI to sharpen and compress your writing, then test the wording before you send it.

Phase 3 · Act confidently

Use AI to rehearse difficult conversations and practice how to read the room, respond, and adjust before doing it live.

How it works

Three phases built around skills you can use the next day.

The exercises put you in practical situations: a cluttered update, a blank page or a difficult conversation. You organize the information, prepare the message and respond under pressure.

Questions

A few things people ask before joining.

Who designed this program?

Wen-Szu Lin, our founder, spent 12 years as a Senior Director at Uber leading teams across Asia, and built this program from the material in his book, Deliver. Read more about his story on the About page.

Does this program teach AI?

Yes. In select exercises, you solve the problem yourself first. Then you use AI to compare approaches, pressure-test your reasoning or rehearse a response. A few exercises focus specifically on using AI well.

Is there a certificate or credential at the end?

Yes. Completing the program gives you two records:

Certificate of completion: A personalized certificate you can download as an image or PDF and keep for your records.

Verified credential: A public, authenticated record with its own credential ID and verification link. You can add it to LinkedIn, include it on your CV or share the link with an employer. Anyone with the link can confirm that the credential is active on The Untaught Lessons website without seeing your email, score or account information.

What if someone misses a live cohort session?

Live sessions are recorded and shared with the cohort. If you miss one, watch the recording and bring your questions to the group chat.
