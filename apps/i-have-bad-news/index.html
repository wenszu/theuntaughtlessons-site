Voice practice

Talk through the conversation.

Voice chat lets you practice responding in the moment. It works in ChatGPT on a computer or phone, and in the Gemini phone app. It takes four steps.

  1. 1

    Paste and send

    Paste the prompt into a new chat and send it as text. The AI takes a moment to read it, then writes a short briefing with your scenario.

    Paste
  2. 2

    Click inside the message box

    In ChatGPT, the voice button stays hidden until your cursor is in the box. Click the box and wait a moment.

    Click here first

  3. 3

    Select the voice button

    ChatGPT: the blue voice button appears at the right of the box.

    Gemini: use the phone app and select the Live button at the bottom right. On a computer, the Gemini microphone only types what you say. It does not talk back, so use ChatGPT there.

    Allow microphone access if asked.

    Then select this button

  4. 4

    Talk, then end it

    Read the briefing, then speak first, as if you are walking up to the person. When you are done, say "Coach, debrief now".

    After the debrief, end the voice chat. Your words stay in the chat as text, so you can paste the transcript request from step 3 of this page.

    Talk

Cannot use voice? You can complete the same practice by typing. The buttons may look different on another device or app version.
