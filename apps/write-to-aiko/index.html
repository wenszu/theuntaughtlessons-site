Welcome back

Your SCQA. Use this to write your email.

Situation

The Olympics is still the world's most recognizable sports property, with unmatched athletes, history, and global reach.

Complication

Recognition is not translating into cultural relevance. Shared moments are fragmenting, short-form consumption favors spectacle over meaning, and questions about legitimacy are making the Games feel less essential and more optional.

Question

How do we restore the Olympics as a rare, shared cultural event without chasing trends or diluting the brand?

Answer

Regain people's attention through (1) ritual design that creates shared moments again, (2) athlete-led story arcs that make the stakes clear over time, and (3) one consistent message and tone that every channel reinforces while controlling commercial signals.

Email draft
