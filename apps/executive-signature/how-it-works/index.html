Executive Signature™

How the score works

The Executive Readiness Score summarizes three traits that research has commonly associated with leadership and executive work. Two more areas describe how you tend to work with people.

There are no right answers

People become effective executives in different ways. Your result describes how you see your habits today. A lower range shows where practice may help most. It says nothing final about your ability or potential.

UTL built this assessment for reflection and development, not to rank you against other people. We deliberately do not report a percentile, because comparing your habits to a wide population would say less about you than looking at your own answers does.
