Perspective taking

Understand their perspective before you respond.

You do not have to agree or compromise. First, find where their view differs from yours. In the Olympic project case, you are Aiko's Chief of Staff and you need Michael Felipe on the team. An AI plays Michael, asks what he is really signaling, and coaches how you respond. Plan for about 20 minutes.

Your switching frameworkHow to switch hats: four questions to ask in every round

To switch hats, take off your own view and put on theirs before you respond. Ask yourself:

  1. 1Name their role and pressures: What do they need to protect? What are they accountable for? What would failure look like for them?
  2. 2Identify what they actually want: Look past the first request. What outcome matters most to them here?
  3. 3Spot where your view and theirs diverge: Where are you assuming they see it the way you do?
  4. 4Adjust your approach: Given what you now know about their world, what would you say differently?
