Privacy notice

How we handle your personal data

Version 2026-10-09. Last updated October 9, 2026.

The short version: we collect only what we need to run our programs and assessments, give you your results, and keep the service safe. We never sell your personal data. We do not use advertising trackers. You can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion of your data at any time.

Who we are

The Untaught Lessons is a learning company founded by Wen-Szu Lin. We run programs such as Think, speak and act like an executive, the Executive Signature assessment, and related practice tools. In this notice, "we" and "us" mean The Untaught Lessons. You can reach us through the contact page or by replying to any email we send you.

What we collect, and when

When you are a member of a program: your name, email address, cohort, the organization that sponsors your access if there is one, your access dates, and when and how you sign in. We also keep your progress through lessons and exercises, the answers you save, your scores, points and certificates, and how you use the workspace, such as the pages you open and the time you spend on a step.

When you take the Executive Signature assessment: your name, email address, age range, career stage, main goal, and optionally your organization, job title and country or region. We keep your answers, your result (scores, band and profile), and a record of which privacy notice you agreed to and when. If you choose to hear from us about programs, we record that as a separate choice.

When you use a form on this site: the waitlist, the contact form, Find your level and the feedback button collect the name, email address and message you type, and the page you were on.

When you ask for AI feedback: the text you write or speak for that exercise is sent to Google's Gemini service, which writes the feedback. We do not send your name or email address with that text.

Through your browser: we store small pieces of information in your browser, such as your sign-in session and a copy of your progress, so the site works and your work is not lost. We also record technical error reports to find and fix problems. We do not use advertising cookies. Services we rely on, such as Vimeo for videos and Cloudflare for delivering the site, may set their own cookies.

Why we use your data

  • To provide the program: to sign you in, save your work, show your progress, issue certificates and send the emails you need, such as sign-in links and your results.
  • To give you results and feedback: to calculate your assessment result and, when you ask, to produce AI feedback.
  • To improve our work: we review combined patterns across many people to improve questions, scoring and guidance. We do not publish individual results.
  • To keep the service safe: to prevent misuse, limit abuse and fix technical problems.
  • To contact you about programs: only if you chose that. You can change your mind at any time.

Who can see your data

Our team: a small number of UTL staff can see member records to run the service and give support. Reading an individual person's saved answers is restricted, and each time it happens it is recorded.

Your sponsoring organization: if your employer, school or another organization pays for your access, its authorized representatives can see your name, email address, progress and completion for the cohorts they support. They do not see your saved answers or your assessment responses.

Service providers: we use other companies to run parts of the service, and they handle your data only for that purpose.

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in service, with data stored in Singapore.
  • Google (Firebase and Gemini): an earlier copy of our records while we finish moving to Supabase, and the AI feedback service.
  • Resend: sends our emails.
  • GitHub and Cloudflare: deliver this website and protect it from abuse.
  • Vimeo and Stripe: Vimeo plays our videos. When paid products are offered, Stripe handles payment, and we never see or store your card number.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How we protect it

Your sign-in uses a one-time email link or your Google or Microsoft account. We never see your password for those accounts. Access to the data is limited by role, our databases enforce who may read each record, and connections to our services are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so if we ever learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you.

How long we keep it

We keep your personal data for as long as you have an account or as long as we need it for the purposes above. If you ask us to delete it, we will, unless the law requires us to keep it. Backup copies may remain for a short time after deletion.

Your choices

  • See your data: ask for a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Correct it: ask us to fix anything that is wrong. You can also change your name and avatar in your account.
  • Delete it: ask us to delete your personal data.
  • Stop emails about programs: use the link in any such email or tell us.
  • Ask a question or make a complaint: we will reply as quickly as we can.

To make any of these requests, use the contact page or reply to an email from us. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act.

Young people

Our programs are written for adults and young professionals. If you are under 18, please check with a parent, guardian or teacher before you share personal details with us.

Changes to this notice

When we change this notice, we will update the date and version at the top of this page. If a change is important, we will also tell members by email.
